Method

Every ERC-8004 registration on chain 4663 was read from the registry, its agent card resolved and validated, and its declared endpoints probed. The grade is mechanical and recomputable: LIVE means the card resolves and an endpoint answers; GHOST means the registration exists and nothing answers; DEAD END means the pointers fail; MALFORMED means the registration itself is incomplete. Grades feed MAD STANDING and the MAD SEAL signing-path verdicts, and the underlying verdicts sit in the public witness ledger.
