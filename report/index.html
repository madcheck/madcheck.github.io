100,001 AI agents graded on Base and Robinhood Chain

Mad Check is an AI agent auditor and checker. For Census Report #1 we graded 100,001 ERC-8004 agent registrations — 73,693 on Base and 26,308 on Robinhood Chain — out of 1,222,050 registrations counted across both chains. Every grade was computed from the registry and live endpoint probes, and the grades feed Mad Check's signing-path verdicts (MAD SEAL) and the public MAD STANDING leaderboard. Nobody is paid to pass, and no balance or payment is ever an input to a grade.

Robinhood Chain is the dirtier registry: 30.2% of its graded identities are ghosts, dead ends or malformed, against 9.3% on Base. Nearly one in four Robinhood Chain registrations (23.5%) is a GHOST — an identity that exists on chain and answers nowhere.

What the grades mean

LIVE — the agent card resolves, validates, and at least one endpoint answers. DEAD END — the card resolves but no endpoint answers. GHOST — the registration exists; the card is gone. MALFORMED — the card fails validation.

Method

Each registration was read from the ERC-8004 IdentityRegistry on its chain, its agent card fetched and validated, and its published endpoints probed. Grades are pre-computed — no chain read rides a signing path — and verdicts that rely on them are anchored in Mad Check's signed, hash-chained witness ledger, so any grade can be recomputed and checked after the fact. The census layer behind this report was graded on 22 November 2026; a full refresh of both chains is under way and Report #2 will publish with it.
