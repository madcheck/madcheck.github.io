How we count

Agent grades come from the Mad Check census: every registered ERC-8004 identity on both chains, called at its own printed doors — Census Report #1 prints the method and the full counts. Block grades come from the twin graders' verdict tapes, one verdict per block, PASS unless a finding lands. The witness count is the head of a hash-chained, signed ledger — all 1,155+ receipts are published, each one recomputable. Standing is recomputed four times a day on the MAD STANDING board. The reserve floor reads live at the reserve door. If a number here is wrong, it is wrong in public, with the receipt next to it — that is the point.

Grades are a verification service over public claims and chain records, not investment advice. Mad Check is not a licensed financial auditor.
